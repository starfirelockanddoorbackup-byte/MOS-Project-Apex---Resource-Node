Medical & TCCC Kit Builder

Tiered medical inventory customized for tactical trauma, austere stabilization, and vulnerable populations.

// Tier 1: Immediate Life-Saving Trauma (TCCC Aligned)

  • Massive Hemorrhage: CAT / SOFTW-T tourniquets, Z-fold hemostatic gauze (QuikClot/Celox), 6" emergency pressure dressings.
  • Airway & Respiration: Nasopharyngeal airway (NPA) with lubricant, vented chest seals (twin-pack).
  • Circulation & Hypothermia: Emergency thermal blankets, trauma shears, casualty cards.

// Tier 2: Austere Stabilization & Infection Control

  • Wound Management: Sterile irrigating saline, chlorhexidine/iodine, non-adherent pads, sterile gauze rolls, surgical tape.
  • Medications: Broad-spectrum oral antibiotics (prescribed protocol), analgesics (NSAIDs, acetaminophen), anti-diarrheals.

// Tier 3: Vulnerable Populations & Special Needs

  • Oxygen & Respiratory: LiFePO4 battery power station sized for oxygen concentrators, manual Bag-Valve-Mask (BVM).
  • Chronic Comorbidity Buffer: 90-day minimum prescription buffer stored in cool, dark environments.
Medical Disclaimer: Advanced interventions and prescription pharmaceuticals require professional clinical oversight.
