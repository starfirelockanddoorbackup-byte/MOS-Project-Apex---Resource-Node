Medical & TCCC Kit Builder

Tiered medical inventory customized for tactical trauma, austere stabilization, and vulnerable populations.

// Tier 1: Immediate Life-Saving Trauma (TCCC Aligned)

  • Massive Hemorrhage: CAT/SOFTW-T tourniquets, Z-fold hemostatic gauze, 6" pressure dressings.
  • Airway & Respiration: Nasopharyngeal airway (NPA), vented chest seals.

// Tier 2: Austere Stabilization & Infection Control

  • Wound Management: Irrigating saline, iodine, sterile gauze, medical tape.
  • Medications: Broad-spectrum antibiotics (prescribed protocol), analgesics.

// Tier 3: Vulnerable Populations & Special Needs

  • Oxygen & Respiratory: LiFePO4 battery backup sized for oxygen concentrators, manual BVM.
  • Chronic Comorbidity Buffer: 90-day minimum prescription buffer in cool storage.
